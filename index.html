Welcome to the Medical Resident Simulation!

This game simulates clinical scenarios to test your medical knowledge and decision-making skills.

To begin, select a case type from the Data panel and click "Start Case".

Work up and treat your patient through the Actions panel. Message the attending with @md and the nurse with @rn (the nurse can ask the patient questions for you).

Every in-game minute (10 seconds) the patient's vitals update. Delays and harmful orders have consequences.

Remember, every action has consequences. Good luck!
